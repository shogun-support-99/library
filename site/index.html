Some study hours
don’t fit a timetable.

AR Smart Library keeps a dedicated reading space open around the clock at Sadabahar Chowk, in Namkum. Exam season, night shifts, a chapter that won’t close at ten. The room stays available at the hour you actually need it.

The library sits on the first floor of Kamla Enclave, beside Katyayni Sweets, so it is easy to point out to an auto driver and easy to find the first time you come. It has been open since 2024 and takes its timings seriously: every day of the week begins and ends with the doors open.

Seats, opening the door at 3 a.m., whether the room is full in the evening: these change week to week. A call before you travel is always worth it.

What this page can
confirm for you.

  • Open twenty-four hours

    All seven days, including Sundays and public holidays, as listed on Google. There is no closing time to plan around.

  • A reading room, not a café

    The library is listed as a library and reading library. People come here to sit with their own material in a quiet, dedicated study environment.

  • A landmark you can name

    Sadabahar Chowk, beside Katyayni Sweets, first floor. If someone in Namkum knows the sweet shop, they know where you are going.

Air conditioning, Wi-Fi, lockers, power backup, parking, washrooms and charging points are not published here, because they are not confirmed. Call the library and ask. It is a two-minute conversation, and it beats arriving with the wrong expectation.
