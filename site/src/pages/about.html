A reading room in Namkum that never closes, and the small set of facts this page is willing to stand behind.

The room, and
the hour.

AR Smart Library keeps a dedicated reading space open around the clock at Sadabahar Chowk, in Namkum. It is listed as a library and as a reading library, and the hours on those listings are unusually simple: open twenty-four hours, seven days a week.

Most study rooms are built around a schedule. That works until the schedule does not: an exam three weeks out, a chapter that only makes sense after midnight, a shift that ends when everything else has closed. A library that never closes removes one decision from the day.

It sits on the first floor of Kamla Enclave, beside Katyayni Sweets. That is not a decorative detail. It is the reason the place is easy to find, easy to describe to an auto driver, and easy to remember the second time you come.

The library has been open since 2024. Public listings carry a 4.7 average on Google and a 4.6 on Justdial. The location page has the map; the facilities page is honest about what is not confirmed.

02Who it is for

Three kinds
of evening.

  • The exam weeks

    When the timetable stops being enough, the day gets longer on its own. A room that stays open means the plan does not have to end at a closing time.

  • The late shift

    Some days finish after ten. If the only quiet hour you have is the one after work, the reading has to happen in that hour, wherever the clock happens to be.

  • The early start

    Others arrive before the city does. What matters then is not a menu of services; it is a door that opens, a light, and a place to sit with the material you brought.

03How this page is written

Nothing here
is guessed.

Everything on this site comes from public business listings, checked on 6 October 2026: the name, the address, the phone number, the 24-hour schedule, the 2024 opening year, and the two rating averages.

Where a detail could not be confirmed, it is left out rather than filled in. That is why there is no price list and no list of in-room facilities. A website that invents a price or a locker is worse than one that sends you to the phone.

Reviews are linked, not copied. The words belong to the people who wrote them.

Ratings and hours can change. The library confirms the current position on +91 70705 49845.
