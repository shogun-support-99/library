02Facilities

What is confirmed,
and what is not.

Three things the public listings support. Everything else is a question for the phone, and this page says so plainly.

Three things
we can say.

  • Open twenty-four hours

    All seven days, including Sundays and public holidays, as listed publicly. There is no closing time to plan around, which is the whole point of the place.

  • A reading room, not a café

    The library is listed as a library and a reading library. People come here to sit with their own material in a dedicated study environment, rather than to order something and leave.

  • A landmark you can name

    Sadabahar Chowk, beside Katyayni Sweets, first floor. If someone in Namkum knows the sweet shop, they know where you are going. The map is on the location page.

—Not published here

The list we
left out.

Air conditioning, Wi-Fi, lockers, CCTV, parking, power backup, washrooms, private cabins, charging points and seat booking are not published on this site, because none of them could be confirmed from a public source.

That is not a suggestion that they are missing. It means this page will not promise something a visitor would discover at the door. The library knows, and the library is one call away.

03Before you go

Six questions,
one call.

  1. 01

    Is a seat free right now, and is it likely to be free at the hour I plan to come?

  2. 02

    What does a membership include, and which plans are open this month?

  3. 03

    Do I need to bring my own books, or is there material in the room?

  4. 04

    Is there somewhere to charge a laptop or a phone?

  5. 05

    How do I get in after midnight: is the door open, or does someone need to let me in?

  6. 06

    Is there anything I should not bring into the reading room?

Questions, not promises. The answers change from month to month, which is exactly why they are not printed on a website.
