Is AR Smart Library really open 24 hours?

Yes. Public business listings show it open 24 hours a day, seven days a week, with the same hours on Sundays.

Where exactly is it?

On the first floor of Kamla Enclave at Sadabahar Chowk, Namkum, beside Katyayni Sweets. The location page has a diagram and a Maps link.

What is the phone number?

+91 70705 49845 is the publicly listed number.

When did the library open?

Public directory information lists the establishment year as 2024.

How do I get a membership?

Call the library. Plans, prices and seat availability are confirmed on the phone rather than on this page. Why no prices are printed.

Is there Wi-Fi, AC, parking or a locker?

These are not confirmed publicly, so they are not claimed here. Ask when you call and you will get a straight answer. The full list of what is and is not confirmed.

Can I come at three in the morning?

The listed hours say yes, every day. If it is your first visit, call ahead so someone knows to expect you.
