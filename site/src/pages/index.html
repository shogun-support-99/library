01About the library

Some study hours
don’t fit a timetable.

AR Smart Library keeps a dedicated reading space open around the clock at Sadabahar Chowk, in Namkum. Exam season, night shifts, a chapter that won’t close at ten. The room stays available at the hour you actually need it.

The library sits on the first floor of Kamla Enclave, beside Katyayni Sweets, so it is easy to point out to an auto driver and easy to find the first time you come. It has been open since 2024 and takes its timings seriously.
