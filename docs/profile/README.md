## What I'm working on

**[AR Smart Library](https://arsmartlibrary-psi.vercel.app/)** — a website for a 24-hour reading library at
Sadabahar Chowk, Namkum, Ranchi. Seven pages, Tailwind v4, a Node build script, and one rule: it may only
say what public listings support. No invented prices, no invented facilities, no copied reviews. The
headline is a live clock reading *“AR Smart Library is open at ⟨time⟩”*, so the promise is shown rather
than claimed.

`Tailwind CSS` · `static site` · `axe: 0 violations at 1440 / 768 / 390` · [code](https://github.com/shogun-support-99/library)

## How I work

- **Static first.** If it can be HTML and a stylesheet, it should be. Nothing to patch, nothing to pay for, nothing that goes down.
- **Local-first data.** Your sets, your library, your notes — on your device, in your browser, exportable.
- **Accessibility is a test, not an intention.** Keyboard paths, reduced motion, and a real audit before launch.
- **Say only what is true.** Especially for a business page. A missing price is better than a stale one.
